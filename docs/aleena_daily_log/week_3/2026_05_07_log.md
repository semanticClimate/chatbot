## What was done

- Ran the FastAPI backend successfully for smoke testing  
- Performed initial chatbot testing to verify whether the system was functioning correctly  
- Tested multiple climate-related queries to check response quality and domain alignment  

### Questions tested:
- What is CUTx Index?  
- What is climate change?  
- What is CO2?  

---

## Observations

- The chatbot was generally able to respond correctly to core climate-related questions  
- No major or blatant functional issues were discovered during initial smoke testing  
- Most tested queries returned acceptable and relevant responses  
- The system appeared to stay mostly within its intended climate-focused knowledge boundaries  

---

## Issues observed

- In rare cases, the chatbot occasionally failed to answer certain valid questions  
- Sometimes responses indicated that the information was unavailable even when the query appeared relevant  
- These failures were inconsistent rather than persistent  

---

## Conclusion

- Overall, smoke testing showed that the chatbot backend was functional  
- The chatbot demonstrated stable performance for most common climate-related prompts  
- Some response inconsistencies were observed, suggesting that further testing may be needed to improve reliability
