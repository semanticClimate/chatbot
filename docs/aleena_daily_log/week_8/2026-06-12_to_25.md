## June 12th – June 25th, 2026

### Codebase Refactoring

During this period, I focused on refactoring the chatbot codebase to improve its overall structure and maintainability.

The existing project had grown organically during rapid development, resulting in files and components being scattered across the repository. To address this, I worked on reorganizing the codebase into a more structured and modular layout.

The refactoring effort included:

* Reorganizing files and directories into a more logical project structure.
* Improving code maintainability by separating related components and reducing clutter.
* Preparing the project for future feature development and easier collaboration.
* Laying the groundwork for integrating additional functionality, such as the IPCC chatbot, without further increasing technical debt.

Although this work did not introduce major user-facing features, it was an important engineering effort that improved the project's organization, readability, and long-term maintainability.
