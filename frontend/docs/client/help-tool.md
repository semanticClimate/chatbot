# Welcome to Climate Academy Chat

This guide will help you navigate and use the chatbot effectively.

## Getting Started

1. **Select your language** — Use the "Chat language" dropdown in the Settings panel at the top. Answers will appear in this language.
2. **Ask a question** — Type your question in the text box at the bottom of the chat panel and press **Send**.
3. **Read the answer** — The assistant responds with text blocks, sometimes with numbered citation chips.

## Using Citations

When the assistant cites a source, you will see numbered buttons (e.g. **1**, **2**, **3**) below the answer block.

- Click a citation number to see the **source details** in the Sources panel.
- The **Student Book** panel on the right will automatically scroll to the referenced passage and highlight it.

## Student Book Panel

The right-hand panel displays the Climate Academy Student Book.

- It loads automatically when the API is connected.
- Click **Reset highlight** to clear any highlighted section and return to the default view.

## Encyclopedia Panel

Below the Student Book you will find the Encyclopedia panel.

- Encyclopedia entries open automatically when the book content links to one.
- This provides additional context from Wikidata-sourced climate science entries.

## Exporting Your Conversation

Under **Export CSV…** in the Settings panel:

- **Download chat** — Saves your full conversation as a CSV file.
- **Server logs** — Downloads the server-side query logs.

## Checking API Health

Click **Check health** in the Settings panel to verify that the backend API is running and responsive.

## Tips

- Ask questions **in the same language** you selected in the dropdown.
- The Student Book text always stays in **English**, regardless of your chat language.
- You can clear the entire conversation by clicking **Clear chat**.
- Sample questions are available under the chat input — expand them for inspiration.
